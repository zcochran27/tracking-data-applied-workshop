# Attributed external illustrations

These are selected original illustrations used to explain published work in the workshop. They are distinct from locally calculated SkillCorner results and the independently trained workshop GNN. Source credit is retained in slide footers and speaker notes; this repository does not claim authorship of the original figures.

- `bekkers/`: Joris Bekkers, PyData London 2025, *Cutting Edge Football Analytics*. Original graph, feature, adjacency and architecture illustrations from the [linked keynote](https://rawcdn.githack.com/UnravelSports/keynotes/main/html/20250607-PyData-London.html). The architecture illustration credits Sahasrabudhe & Bekkers (2023). See `bekkers/attribution.json` for asset URLs.
- `skillcorner/`: original validation and physical-speed examples from [SkillCorner’s Peak Velocity article](https://skillcorner.com/articles/skillcorner-releases-new-peak-velocity-metric), 11 June 2026. Used for official PSV-99/Peak Velocity definitions and published examples, not to imply local reproduction of proprietary processing.
- `research/`: selected result figures from SoccerMap, DEFCON, counterattack GNNs, TacticAI and GenTac. `research/attribution.json` records original URLs and figure/panel context. DEFCON Figure 3 and GenTac Figure 5a are cropped to their result panels; numeric values are unchanged. All original research figures retain their authors’ rights and source terms.

Full papers and downloaded HTML/PDF research caches are not published. General temporal-network architecture slides are excluded from the current deck.
